Recovery Ledger privacy policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

Your records stay on your device

Recovery Ledger stores case titles, account nicknames, providers, status, checklists, follow-up dates, and timeline notes in its local app database. Digital Damage does not operate a server for this app, include analytics or advertising SDKs, or receive your cases.

Exports and backups

You can export readable JSON backups and PDF case summaries using Apple’s file and share interfaces. Anyone with access to an exported file may read it. Your device backup settings may include app data; those services are controlled by your device and backup provider.

Device authentication

Optional authentication uses Apple’s device authentication framework. Recovery Ledger never receives biometric data or your device passcode.

External websites

Official recovery links open the provider’s website. That provider’s privacy policy applies there. The app does not receive your recovery credentials or results.

Deleting records

Delete one case from its detail screen, or all cases from Settings. Delete exported files and device backup copies separately.

Keep secrets elsewhere

Do not record passwords, recovery codes, MFA seeds, private keys, cookies, or tokens. Recovery Ledger is an organizer, not a password manager.
